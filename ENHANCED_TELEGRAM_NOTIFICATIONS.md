## 🎯 What You Get

### For Each Notification:
1. **Date** - The exact date tickets are available
2. **Ticket Name** - Full name (e.g., "Musei Vaticani - Biglietti d'ingresso")
3. **Visitors** - Number of people (e.g., 2)
4. **Language** - If guided tour (e.g., "ENG")
5. **ALL Time Slots** - Every single available time
6. **Preferred Times** - Your favorites highlighted with ⭐
7. **Total Count** - How many slots total
8. **Booking Link** - Direct link to book
9. **Check Time** - When we found the tickets
10. **Method** - How we checked (search_api)

## 🔔 When You Receive Notifications

Notifications are sent when:
- ✅ Tickets go from SOLD_OUT → AVAILABLE (state change)
- ✅ First time tickets become available (optional)
- ❌ NOT when tickets are still available (no change)
- ❌ NOT when tickets are still sold out (no change)

**Spam Protection:**
- 1-hour cooldown per ticket/date
- Prevents duplicate alerts
- Only alerts on actual state changes

## 📝 How to Configure Preferred Times

### Via Dashboard:
1. Edit your task
2. Set `preferred_times` field
3. Example: `["08:00", "09:00", "10:00"]`
4. Save

### Via Telegram Bot:
1. When adding a monitor
2. Select preferred times from the menu
3. Or click "Skip" if you don't have preferences

### Result:
- Preferred times appear first in notifications
- Marked with ⭐ star emoji
- Easy to spot your favorite times
